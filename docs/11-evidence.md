# Evidence and references

Checked November 15, 2026. These sources inform decisions and initial hypotheses. They do not validate this product, its price, its speed or its effect on learning. Recheck changing model names, data terms and SDK behavior during M0.

## Research that changes how the tutor behaves

| Evidence | What it supports in this product | Limit to preserve |
|---|---|---|
| [IES What Works Clearinghouse, Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/1/Published) | Alternate worked solutions with new problems, connect graphics and explanations, link concrete and abstract representations and revisit material. Put a label on the exact feature being discussed. | The guide assigns different evidence levels to different recommendations. It does not validate our UI or a fixed hint schedule. |
| [Roediger & Karpicke, Test-enhanced learning, 2006](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16507066/) | Ask learners to retrieve an idea before seeing a saved explanation. Record a delayed attempt separately from immediate performance. | The study tested retention of studied prose; transfer to statistical problem solving must be measured. |
| [Bastani et al., Generative AI without guardrails can harm learning, PNAS](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2422633122) | In a high-school math field experiment, a general GPT interface improved practice grades while subsequent unassisted grades were about 17% lower than control. Guardrails using teacher-designed guidance largely mitigated that deficit. Test for unassisted transfer and model appropriate help. | This was a specific setting and intervention. It neither proves our tutor improves learning nor implies withholding all direct explanations. |
| [Kestin et al., AI tutoring outperforms in-class active learning, Scientific Reports](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-97652-6) | A carefully designed, self-paced tutor achieved stronger immediate outcomes in the studied college physics lessons. Invest in instructional design, pacing and feedback as well as the model. | Specific lessons and students; it is not a comparison of every AI tutor with human tutors or proof of long-term retention. |
| [Brown University, Generative AI in Teaching and Learning committee report](https://provost.brown.edu/sites/default/files/GAITL_Committee_Report_FNL.pdf) | Among respondents, 73% in online master’s programs reported paid AI subscriptions versus 17% of undergraduates. Supports testing the adult online learner first. | Voluntary single-campus responses about AI subscriptions, not willingness to buy this application. |
| [Deloitte, 2026 U.S. back-to-school parent survey](https://www.deloitte.com/us/en/about/press-room/deloitte-back-to-school-spending-holds-steady.html) | Some parents intend to buy AI tutoring or camps and many worry about overreliance. Test independent progress and interest through adult interviews. | Intent combines two categories and is not a completed purchase. |
| [StudyFetch Tutor Me setup](https://www.studyfetch.com/docs/docs/tutor-me/create-a-tutor-me-session) · [MIT RAISE PyTutor](https://raise.mit.edu/research/research-projects/pytutor/) | Course tutoring, screen sharing, a collaborative whiteboard and code help have precedents. Differentiate via exact course evidence, low-friction handoff and verified learning in the student's work. | Documented features and project descriptions are not head-to-head benchmarks. The pilot must demonstrate the difference. |

## Assumptions that need direct measurement

No study here establishes sub-two-second chart display on a learner’s device or that the product makes a good teacher. The language model is not a numerical verifier, and an embedding match is not proof that a claim is supported. The $19 pass, cohort size, variable cost ceiling, coaching defaults and release thresholds are founder-facing experiment parameters. Validate them using the [acceptance and pilot plan](09-evaluation.md).
